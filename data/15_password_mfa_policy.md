# Password and MFA Policy — Internal Staff

This policy applies to every Libra Assist employee and contractor with access to internal systems.

## Password Requirements
* Minimum length: **12 characters**, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol.
* Passwords must be rotated every **90 days** for standard accounts, and every **30 days** for privileged/administrator accounts.
* A password may not repeat any of the last **10** passwords used on that account.
* Shared accounts (generic logins used by more than one person) are prohibited without a documented, IT-Security-approved exception.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* MFA is **mandatory** on all systems that touch customer data, without exception.
* Approved factors are the corporate authenticator app or a hardware security key. SMS-based codes are not accepted as the second factor for staff accounts.
* Losing the device enrolled for MFA must be reported to IT Security **within 1 hour** of discovery, so the factor can be revoked.

## Enforcement
Accounts found without MFA enabled are automatically suspended by the identity system until the employee re-enrolls with their manager and IT Security present.
