# Device and Remote Access Policy — Internal Staff

## Company Devices
1. Full-disk encryption and the corporate endpoint-protection agent must be active before a device is allowed to enroll in company email or VPN.
2. Screens must lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity.
3. Only software approved by IT may be installed; unapproved software is removed automatically on the next compliance scan.

## Personal Devices (BYOD)
* Personal devices may access company email and calendar only through the managed mobile-app container, never through the native mail app.
* Personal devices may never access Confidential or Restricted systems (see the data classification policy) — only Internal-level resources.

## Remote Access
* All remote access to internal systems goes through the corporate VPN with MFA — direct exposure of internal services to the public internet is prohibited.
* VPN sessions expire after **8 hours** and require re-authentication.
* Working from a public, unsecured Wi-Fi network is permitted only through the VPN; connecting to internal systems on public Wi-Fi without the VPN active is a reportable policy violation.
