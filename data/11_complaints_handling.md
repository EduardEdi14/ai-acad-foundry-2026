# Filing and Resolving Customer Complaints

Customers may file formal complaints through the following official channels:
* The complaint form within the mobile application.
* By email at `complaints@libra-assist-demo.com`.
* In writing at any physical branch location.

## Resolution Deadlines
* The standard resolution deadline is **15 business days** from the registration date.
* In exceptional cases involving international card transactions, the deadline may be extended up to a maximum of **35 business days**, with the customer being notified in writing.
