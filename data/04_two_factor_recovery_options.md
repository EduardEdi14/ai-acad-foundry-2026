# Recovering Access After Losing Your 2FA Device

If you lose the phone or authenticator app used for Two-Factor Authentication, you still have three ways back into your account.

## Recovery Options, in Order of Speed
1. **One-time recovery key** — enter the key you saved during enrollment on the "Lost my device" screen. Access is restored immediately.
2. **Backup SMS code** — if a backup phone number was registered, request a code by SMS; delivery is instant but the option must have been enabled beforehand.
3. **Branch identity verification** — without a recovery key or backup number, you must visit a branch in person with a valid ID; 2FA is reset within **1 business day** of the visit.

A phone call to the call centre alone cannot reset 2FA — this is by design, so that a stolen phone number cannot be used to talk your way past account protection.
