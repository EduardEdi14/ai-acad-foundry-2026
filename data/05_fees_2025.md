# Current Account Fee Schedule - Valid in 2025

*(Note: This schedule was replaced on January 1st, 2026)*

* Monthly account maintenance fee: 10 LEI / month.
* Interbank transfer via mobile app (SEPA Lei): 2 LEI per transaction.
* Cash withdrawal from non-network ATMs: 1.5% of the amount (minimum 5 LEI).
