# Opening a 100% Online Bank Account

The onboarding process takes approximately 10 minutes and requires a valid national ID card.

## Steps to Follow:
1. Download the Libra Assist mobile app from the AppStore or Google Play.
2. Tap the **Become a Customer** button.
3. Enter your phone number and email address, then verify the one-time passwords received.
4. Take a photo of both sides of your national ID card.
5. Complete video verification using facial recognition (liveness test).
6. Sign the master agreement using the simple electronic signature generated inside the app.
