# Customer Notification for Detected Suspicious Activity - 2025

*(Note: This policy was replaced on January 1st, 2026)*

* When the fraud engine flags a transaction as suspicious, the customer was notified by SMS and email within **48 hours** of detection.
* The flagged transaction remained pending (not settled) until the customer confirmed or denied it, or until the 48-hour window elapsed.
* No push notification was sent inside the mobile app under this policy.
