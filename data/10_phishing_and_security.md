# Account Security and Data Protection

Libra Assist will NEVER request the following information via SMS, email, or telephone calls:
* Your card PIN code.
* Your password for Internet Banking access.
* One-Time Passwords (OTP) received via SMS.
* Complete card credentials (16-digit number, expiration date, CVV2/CVC2 code on the back).

If you have entered data on a suspicious website, immediately block your card from the mobile app and call our emergency line at **021.999.000**.
