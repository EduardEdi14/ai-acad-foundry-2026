# Reporting an Unauthorized Transaction

An unauthorized transaction is any payment or withdrawal you did not make and did not approve, including one made after your card details were phished.

## Step-by-Step: What To Do Immediately
1. **Freeze the card first** — open the mobile app, go to **Cards** -> select the card -> **Freeze card**. This takes effect instantly and stops any further use.
2. Call the emergency line at **021.999.000**, available 24/7, to report the transaction and confirm the freeze.
3. Do not uninstall or delete the mobile banking app — the fraud team may need your device logs.
4. File a written dispute from the app (**Cards** -> **Dispute a transaction**) or at any branch, within **13 business days** of the transaction date. This deadline is a regulatory limit for a refund to be guaranteed while the investigation is open.
5. You will receive a provisional credit for the disputed amount within 10 business days of filing, pending the investigation outcome.

## What the Bank Needs From You
* The approximate date, time, and amount of the transaction.
* Whether you still have physical possession of the card.
* Whether you entered any card details or codes on a website or app in the days before the transaction.

Libra Assist will never ask you to confirm your PIN, CVV2/CVC2, or full card number over the phone to process a fraud report — the agent already has this information on file.
