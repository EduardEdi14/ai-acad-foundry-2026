# Reimbursement for Confirmed Unauthorized Transactions

Once a reported unauthorized transaction is confirmed as fraud (not a customer-authorized payment), reimbursement follows a fixed timeline.

## Timeline and Limits
* Confirmed fraud is reimbursed within **10 business days** of confirmation, credited to the same account the funds were taken from.
* Customer liability is capped at **50 EUR** for transactions confirmed as fraud before the card was reported lost or stolen.
* Liability is **0 EUR** for any transaction confirmed after the card was reported lost, stolen, or compromised.
* Claims must be filed within **13 months** of the transaction date to remain eligible for reimbursement.

Reimbursement timelines assume the card was already frozen per the fraud reporting procedure; an unfrozen, still-active card under dispute may take longer to resolve.
