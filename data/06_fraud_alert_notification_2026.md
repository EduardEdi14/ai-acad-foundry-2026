# Customer Notification for Detected Suspicious Activity - 2026

* When the fraud engine flags a transaction as suspicious, the customer is now notified in **real time** — a push notification is sent to the mobile app within **60 seconds** of detection, in addition to SMS and email.
* The flagged transaction is held for a maximum of **15 minutes** awaiting an in-app confirm/deny response before a specialist reviews it manually.
* Customers who disabled push notifications fall back to the 2025 SMS/email timeline (48 hours) until notifications are re-enabled.
