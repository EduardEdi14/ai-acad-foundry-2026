# Blocking and Unblocking Credit and Debit Cards

A payment card issued by Libra Assist can be blocked automatically by the security system or manually by the customer.

## Reasons for Automatic Blocking
* Entering an incorrect PIN code 3 consecutive times at any POS or ATM.
* Detection of suspicious transactions by the anti-fraud module.
* Exceeding the daily limit for offline attempts.

## Step-by-Step: How to Unblock a Card Blocked for Incorrect PIN
1. Open the Libra Assist mobile application.
2. Navigate to the **Cards** section -> Select the affected card.
3. Tap on **Card Settings** -> **Reset PIN Counter**.
4. Confirm your identity using biometric authentication (Fingerprint or FaceID).
5. Enter the OTP code received via SMS on your registered phone number.
6. The card will be unblocked instantly and you can retry your transaction at an ATM.
