# Limits and Administrative Fees for Mortgage Loans

Financial ceilings for mortgage loans are regulated as follows:

* The maximum granted amount is **85%** of the appraised property value (requires a minimum down payment of 15%).
* The loan file analysis fee is 500 LEI, payable only upon loan approval.
* Property appraisal fee is 600 LEI for apartments and 800 LEI for houses/villas.
