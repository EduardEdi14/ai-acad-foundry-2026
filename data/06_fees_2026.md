# Official Current Account Fee Schedule - Valid for 2026

* Monthly account maintenance fee: **0 LEI** (Free if a minimum income of 2,000 LEI/month is deposited, otherwise 12 LEI/month).
* Interbank transfer via mobile app (SEPA Lei): **0 LEI** (Free).
* Cash withdrawal from non-network ATMs: 1% of the amount (minimum 3 LEI).
* Maximum daily transfer limit via mobile application: 50,000 LEI.
