# What To Do If You Suspect Your Personal Data Was Exposed

If you believe your personal data held by Libra Assist (name, ID number, address, account details) may have been exposed — for example, through a data breach notification from another company reusing your information, or a leaked document — follow these steps.

## Step-by-Step
1. Do not wait for unauthorized transactions to appear before acting — data exposure and financial fraud can happen separately.
2. Change your Internet Banking password immediately, even if you have not noticed suspicious activity.
3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication if it is not already active.
4. Report the suspected exposure through **Security** -> **Report a Data Concern** in the mobile app, or by email at `dataprotection@libra-assist-demo.com`.
5. The Data Protection team acknowledges every report within **2 business days** and provides a written outcome within **30 calendar days**, in line with data protection regulations.
6. If financial loss occurs as a result, also follow the fraud reporting procedure to freeze the affected card or account.
