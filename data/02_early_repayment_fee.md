# Fees for Early Repayment of Mortgages and Personal Loans

Customers may perform partial or full early repayments for mortgage or personal loans at any point during the contract duration.

## Fee Structure
* During the **fixed interest rate** period: the early repayment fee is **exactly 1%** of the early repaid amount if the remaining period of the contract is greater than one year.
* If the remaining period of the contract is less than one year, the fee is **exactly 0.5%**.
* During the **variable interest rate** period: the early repayment fee is **0%** (zero).
