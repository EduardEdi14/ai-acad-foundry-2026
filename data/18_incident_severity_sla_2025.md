# Incident Severity and Response SLA — Valid in 2025

*(Note: This SLA was replaced on January 1st, 2026 — see the 2026 version for current times.)*

| Severity | Definition | Escalate to CISO within | Customer notification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Severity 1** | Active customer financial loss or system-wide compromise | 4 hours | Within 24 hours |
| **Severity 2** | Compromised single account, no confirmed loss yet | 24 hours | Within 48 hours |
| **Severity 3** | Suspicious activity, contained, no compromise confirmed | 3 business days | Not required |
| **Severity 4** | Policy violation, no security impact | 5 business days | Not required |
