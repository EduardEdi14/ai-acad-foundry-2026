# Incident Severity and Response SLA — Valid for 2026

Following two Severity 1 incidents in 2025 where the 4-hour escalation window was judged too slow, the escalation times were tightened for 2026.

| Severity | Definition | Escalate to CISO within | Customer notification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Severity 1** | Active customer financial loss or system-wide compromise | **1 hour** | Within 12 hours |
| **Severity 2** | Compromised single account, no confirmed loss yet | **8 hours** | Within 24 hours |
| **Severity 3** | Suspicious activity, contained, no compromise confirmed | 2 business days | Not required |
| **Severity 4** | Policy violation, no security impact | 5 business days | Not required |
