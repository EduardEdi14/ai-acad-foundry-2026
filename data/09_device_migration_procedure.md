# Moving Libra Assist to a New Phone

Follow this procedure whenever you switch to a new phone, to avoid both lockout and impersonation risk.

## Step-by-Step
1. On your **old device**, open **Security** -> **Manage Devices** and note the device name so you can confirm it disappears later.
2. Download the Libra Assist app on the **new device** and log in with your existing username and password.
3. Complete Two-Factor Authentication using your authenticator app or recovery key — this cannot be skipped on a new device.
4. Re-enroll biometrics (fingerprint/FaceID) on the new device; the old device's biometric data does not transfer.
5. From **Security** -> **Manage Devices**, remove the old device from the trusted device list once you have confirmed the new one works.
6. Do not install Libra Assist on the old device again without repeating identity verification, even for a "quick reinstall."

Never share a Two-Factor Authentication code with anyone claiming to help you migrate devices, including someone claiming to be Libra Assist support — the bank never asks for this code.
