# Enrolling in Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-Factor Authentication adds a second verification step (an app-generated code or push approval) on top of your password.

## When 2FA Is Required
* Mandatory for all online banking accounts with a linked debit or credit card.
* Mandatory before the first payment to a new (never-used) beneficiary.
* Optional, but strongly recommended, for read-only access such as balance checks.

## How to Enroll
1. Open **Security** -> **Two-Factor Authentication** in the mobile app.
2. Choose an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator) or SMS-based codes.
3. Scan the QR code shown on screen with your chosen authenticator app.
4. Enter the 6-digit code generated by the app to confirm enrollment.
5. Store the one-time recovery key shown on screen somewhere safe — it is shown only once.

See the Two-Factor Authentication Recovery Options document for what to do if you lose access to your authenticator device.
