# Term Deposits in LEI and EUR

Interest rates are fixed for the full term of the deposit and paid at maturity:

## LEI Term Deposits
* 3-month term deposit: **5.25%** per annum.
* 6-month term deposit: **5.80%** per annum.
* 12-month term deposit: **6.20%** per annum.

The minimum deposit creation amount is **500 LEI** or **200 EUR**. Interest compounding is not available for 3-month deposits.
