# Recovering a Compromised Online Banking Account

If you suspect someone else has accessed your Libra Assist online banking account (unexpected login alert, unrecognized device, or settings you did not change), act immediately.

## Step-by-Step: Securing Your Account
1. Open the Libra Assist mobile application and go to **Security** -> **Active Sessions**.
2. Tap **Log Out All Devices** to terminate every active session except your current one.
3. Change your Internet Banking password immediately from **Security** -> **Change Password**.
4. Enable or re-confirm Two-Factor Authentication if it was disabled.
5. Review the **Recent Transactions** list for the last 30 days and flag anything you do not recognize.
6. Call the 24/7 fraud line at **021.999.000** to have a specialist confirm the account is secured and, if needed, freeze it pending investigation.

Do not wait for a callback before completing steps 1-3 — those can be done immediately from the app, without assistance.
