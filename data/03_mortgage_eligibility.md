# Eligibility for the Standard Libra Mortgage Loan

To be eligible for a mortgage loan, the applicant must cumulatively meet the following conditions:

* Age between 21 and 65 years at the date of loan maturity.
* Employment tenure of at least 3 uninterrupted months at the current employer and a total work experience of at least 12 months.
* Minimum proven net income of 3,500 LEI/month.
* The maximum allowable debt-to-income ratio is 40% of the net monthly income for LEI loans.
