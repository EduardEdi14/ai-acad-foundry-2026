# Public Wi-Fi and Shared Device Rules

Libra Assist online banking must never be accessed under the following conditions:
* Over open, password-free public Wi-Fi (airports, cafes, hotels) without an active VPN.
* On a shared or public computer without using the "Sign out of all sessions" option before leaving.
* With the app's "Remember this device" option enabled on any device you do not personally own.

If you must check your balance while on public Wi-Fi, use mobile data or a trusted personal hotspot instead — never enter your password over an unsecured network.
