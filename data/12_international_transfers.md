# SWIFT and SEPA International Payments

## SEPA Transfers (EUR)
* All Euro SEPA payments made to countries within the European Economic Area are processed on the same day (Instant SEPA) and carry a fee of **0 EUR**.

## SWIFT Transfers (Other Currencies - USD, GBP)
* Processing fee: **0.15%** of the amount (minimum 15 EUR, maximum 150 EUR).
* FX margin applied above the national central bank rate is **0.5%** during trading hours (Monday-Friday 09:00 - 17:00).
